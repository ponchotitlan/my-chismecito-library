## Abstract
- **Version:** 5
- **Format:** Plain text (Markdown with links)

Network operations teams drown in complexity: incident triage, compliance audits, and inventory housekeeping pile up while tribal knowledge scatters across wikis and tickets. AI agents could help, but without proper guardrails, they become yet another unmanaged system.

AI platform engineering provides the foundation: authentication, authorization, observability, and controlled access for agents and tools. At its core, it answers "who can do what, with which tools" on your infrastructure. Applied to networks, this means specific operators and agents performing defined tasks (triage, audit, inventory check) using controlled access to network APIs and data sources, with every action logged and attributable. This talk introduces [CAIPE](https://caipe.io/), a [CNCF Sandbox project](https://landscape.cncf.io/?group=projects-and-products&view-mode=card&item=provisioning--automation-configuration--caipe#app-definition-and-development--application-definition-image-build) assembling fully FOSS components with complete plumbing for robust AI platform engineering. Using CAIPE, we'll demonstrate automated incident triage querying indexed playbooks and live network data, compliance audits comparing configurations against policy, and misalignment detection across inventories.

You'll leave with deployment patterns for secure, observable AI-assisted network operations and access to the full demo setup in a public repository. Aimed at network engineers and SREs exploring responsible automation.
