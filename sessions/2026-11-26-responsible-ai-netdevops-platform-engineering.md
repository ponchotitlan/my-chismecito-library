## Abstract
- **Version:** 6
- **Format:** Plain text (Markdown with links)

Network operations teams drown in complexity: incident triage, compliance audits, and inventory housekeeping pile up while tribal knowledge scatters across wikis and tickets. AI agents could help, but without guardrails they become yet another unmanaged system.

AI platform engineering provides the foundation: authentication, authorization, observability, and controlled access for agents and tools. It answers "who can do what, with which tools": named operators and agents run defined tasks through controlled access to network northbound surfaces, with every action logged and attributable.

We introduce [CAIPE](https://caipe.io/), a [CNCF Sandbox project](https://landscape.cncf.io/) for AI platform engineering built from swappable FOSS components, and show how it handles networking chores responsibly. A quick demo, all on a live network topology, covers incident triage over indexed playbooks and live network data, compliance audits, and misalignment detection across actors and inventories.

You'll leave with deployment patterns and the full demo in a public repository. For network engineers and SREs exploring responsible AI-assisted automation.
